## Prompt final

Create a single polished photorealistic editorial still-life photograph for the hero of an Indonesian university investment literacy portal, GIBEI UISI. Portrait 4:5 composition, restrained academic editorial art direction. A light off-white desk with a large open graph-paper notebook, subtle hand-drawn non-numeric blue line and bar study sketches (illustrative learning marks, no actual financial data), a navy blue hardcover book partly underneath, a blue pencil and simple silver pen, a small calculator partly visible. Warm natural daylight from the left, soft realistic shadows, tactile paper and fabric, deep navy and muted teal details, subtle pale limestone background. Camera overhead at a slight angle, closely composed, intentional negative space, sophisticated university publication photography. No people, no laptops, no screens, no dashboard UI, no legible words, no logos, no text overlay, no currency symbols, no prices, no glossy 3D objects or coins, no money trees, no neon. This is an illustrative scene, not documentation of an actual official event. Output the photograph only.
