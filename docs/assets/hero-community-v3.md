- Tool: imagegen built-in.

## Prompt desktop

Create one photorealistic editorial illustration photograph for GIBEI UISI, an Indonesian university capital-market education community. Panoramic landscape aspect ratio 3:1, wide horizontal banner composition. Four Indonesian university students in their early twenties, two men and two women, one woman wearing a simple navy hijab, seated closely around a shared study table in a bright contemporary university learning space. They are naturally discussing a printed financial literacy worksheet together, with one student thoughtfully pointing at a simple abstract bar chart on paper. A silver laptop is angled away from the camera, so no screen is readable. Blue and navy notebooks, a few sheets of paper, modest everyday campus clothing in navy, blue, white and pale grey. Authentic candid body language, thoughtful and approachable, not cheering, no staged handshake, nobody looking at camera. Composition: the four faces and their shared gesture are grouped across the middle horizontal band; all faces visible, camera at seated eye level, crop from mid torso upward with the tabletop along the lower edge, enough breathing room at left and right. Soft daylight, pale cool blue and white interior, subtle blurred shelves in background, natural realistic skin texture, sophisticated Indonesian university editorial photography. No text, no logos, no badges, no branded uniform, no readable financial prices, no money, no coins, no giant floating graphics. This is a clearly illustrative generated campus collaboration scene, not a photograph of actual GIBEI UISI members or an official event. Output only the image.

## Prompt mobile

Create a responsive mobile companion image for the supplied panoramic illustration. Use the supplied image as the reference for the same four fictional Indonesian university students, faces, clothing, blue-white palette, university library space, daylight, and collaborative finance-learning moment. Recompose the scene as a compact 4:3 landscape photograph, camera slightly farther back, all four students seated close together around a small table. Every person's full face and head must be visible with breathing room, all four people grouped centrally within the frame, no person at an extreme edge. Show them naturally discussing the worksheet and laptop, not facing camera. Keep financial charts abstract without readable labels, no branding, no text, no logos. This is an illustrative generated scene, not official event documentation. Preserve natural anatomy, realistic hands and candid editorial photography. Output only the 4:3 photo.
